About

Welcome to The Roundup Games, the student-run games section of Brophy's student newspaper The Roundup. Each crossword and word search is made and edited by humans, and all of the questions in the three always available games has been made and reviewed by humans as well.

Similar to The Roundup's newspaper portion, any opinions represented on The Roundup's platforms are those of the student authors and do not reflect the views of any faculty, the Brophy administration, or Brophy College Preparatory.

All original content on The Roundup Games is made by Carter Kasarjian '30, staff writer for The Roundup Club, and edited by Nathan Bass '27, the entertainment editor for The Roundup.

The framework and design of The Roundup Games is by Carter Kasarjian '30 in collaboration with Claude Code. View the GitHub repository here.

The Roundup and The Roundup Games is a student publication of Brophy College Preparatory. Copyright 2026 Brophy College Preparatory's The Roundup. No material may be used without permission from the editors and adviser. Contact the editors of The Roundup Games directly via the bug report and contact form, or contact the editors and adviser of The Roundup as a whole at roundup@brophybroncos.org.

Enjoy!
