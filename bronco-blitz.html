Persistent Game

Bronco Blitz

30 seconds on the clock. Answer A/B/C/D trivia as fast as you can — chain correct answers for a growing multiplier, and answer quickly for a speed bonus. Every correct answer also buys back time (1s × your streak multiplier, clock capped at 30s). Miss one and you're locked out for 3 seconds.

Play

0:30

You've got 30 seconds. Every correct answer is worth 100 points, multiplied by your current streak (up to 3x) — plus a speed bonus for answering fast — and it adds 1 second per point of that multiplier back to the clock (up to a 30-second max). A wrong answer breaks your streak and locks you out for 3 seconds while the clock keeps running. Score as many points as you can before time's up.
