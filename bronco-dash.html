Bronco Dash (BETA)

Answer questions to sprint down the track — but a pace line is always creeping up behind you. Reach the finish line before it catches you.

You start at tick 30 of 60, right on the start line. A red pace line trails you and creeps closer on its own — if it catches you, you lose.
